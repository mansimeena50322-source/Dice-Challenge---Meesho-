## Overview

This project was developed as part of the **Dice Challenge - Meesho**, focusing on identifying growth opportunities and proposing product-led solutions to improve customer acquisition, conversion, and repeat purchases.

The analysis combined **user insights, market research, product funnel analysis, and the RICE prioritization framework** to evaluate potential growth levers and identify high-impact opportunities.

---

## Problem Statement

The objective was to identify product and growth opportunities that could improve Meesho's customer acquisition and conversion while strengthening engagement among users in Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities.

The case study focuses on:

* Reducing customer acquisition cost (CAC)
* Increasing repeat purchases
* Improving conversion rates
* Understanding user friction points
* Identifying scalable growth opportunities
* Prioritizing initiatives based on potential impact and feasibility

---

## Business Problem

Meesho has a large user base across Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities, where purchasing behavior is strongly influenced by regional content, social recommendations, and creator-led discovery.

Key challenges identified included:

* High customer acquisition costs
* Dependence on external social platforms for product discovery
* Friction between product discovery and checkout
* Limited integration between social content and shopping
* Difficulty converting creator-driven product discovery into completed purchases
* Need for stronger repeat-purchase behavior

## Tier-2/3 Creator Strategy

The analysis focused on the role of **regional creators and micro-influencers** in reaching customers outside major metropolitan areas.

Key observations included:

* Regional-language content can improve product discoverability
* Micro-influencers can provide more localized and relatable recommendations
* Creator-led product discovery can help establish trust among users
* Tier-2/3 creators can provide access to highly targeted communities

Based on the analysis, a creator-led acquisition strategy was proposed to improve the efficiency of customer acquisition.

The strategy was estimated to have the potential to:

* Reduce CAC by approximately **45%**
* Improve repeat purchases by **4.1x**

These figures represent case-study estimates/benchmarks rather than measured results from a live Meesho deployment.

---

## User Friction Points

The product funnel was analyzed to identify major points of friction between product discovery and purchase.

Key friction areas included:

### Product Discovery

Users may discover products through social content but need to switch platforms or manually search for the product.

### Platform Switching

Moving between social platforms and the shopping platform introduces additional steps in the purchase journey.

### Product Search

Users may need to identify and search for a product after discovering it through external content.

### Checkout

Additional navigation and checkout steps can create friction between product interest and completed purchase.

---

## Growth Lever Prioritization

More than **12 growth levers** were evaluated using the **RICE framework**.

RICE evaluates initiatives using four components:

* **Reach**
* **Impact**
* **Confidence**
* **Effort**

The framework was used to compare potential initiatives systematically and identify opportunities with high potential reach and impact relative to implementation effort.

Among the evaluated opportunities, **Seamless Shopping Experience** was identified as a quick-win opportunity in the case analysis.

The opportunity was estimated to have a potential reach of approximately **25M users per month**.

---

## Proposed Solution

The proposed solution was a **seamless in-platform social shopping experience** that reduces the gap between content discovery and product purchase.

### Proposed User Flow

```text
Social Content / Creator
          |
          v
   Product Discovery
          |
          v
   Product Information
          |
          v
   In-Platform Shopping
          |
          v
      Checkout
          |
          v
     Purchase
```

Instead of requiring users to leave the discovery experience and manually search for products, relevant products could be surfaced directly within the shopping journey.

---

## Key Product Features

### Creator-Led Product Discovery

Enable creators to showcase products and connect content directly with relevant product listings.

### In-Platform Product Access

Allow users to access product information without unnecessary platform switching.

### Seamless Checkout

Reduce the number of steps between product discovery and purchase.

### Regional Content

Support regional languages and localized creator content to improve relevance for Tier-2 and Tier-3 audiences.

### Social Proof

Use creator recommendations and user-generated content to strengthen product discovery and purchase consideration.

## Key Takeaways

* Tier-2/3 creator ecosystems can provide targeted and localized customer acquisition opportunities.
* Regional-language content and micro-influencers can improve relevance and trust during product discovery.
* Reducing friction between discovery and checkout can address a major conversion bottleneck.
* The RICE framework provides a structured approach to evaluating multiple growth opportunities.
* A seamless in-platform shopping experience can connect social discovery directly with product purchase.
* The proposed initiatives were evaluated using estimated and benchmarked impacts rather than live production data.
